# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Be respectful and constructive
- Assume good intent
- Accept feedback gracefully
- Focus on what's best for the community and users

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Publishing private information without consent
- Any behavior that creates an unsafe environment

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (GitHub issues, PRs, discussions, and community channels).

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, or PRs that violate this Code of Conduct.
